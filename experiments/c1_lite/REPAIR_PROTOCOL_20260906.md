This protocol precedes the new experiments. Historical A/B results and all

## Contract and stopping rules

- Retain the fixed four demo events and their original windows, including every
  source breakpoint. Do not change windows, tolerances or events based on gains.
- The first implementation rejects any retained degenerate face incident to the
  replacement interface. Remote retained defects may remain unchanged, but are
  not claimed to be repaired. This rule applies equally to E0 and all other cases.
- Source owners are recorded as a many-to-one relation to emitted oriented faces.
  Final array indices are not persistent source identities. Raw and extra_smooth
  are different emission/geometry models and must not share unsupported proofs.
- Ideal rational affine-branch certificates are distinct from actual binary32
  arithmetic certificates. A successful ideal audit does not admit a runtime plan.
- Preserve the ideal local graph preconditions. Certify only source-declared
  shared contact; no blanket non-strict separation or skipped shared neighbours.
- Source/ownership, original and replacement disks, interface incidence and
  links, complete temporal schedule, event isolation, runtime representation,
  rounding/contact safety and unchanged exterior are distinct admission gates.
- Missing, UNKNOWN or REJECT gates forbid commit. Continuous-window fallback is
  the identical baseline for the entire window, INCLUDING its root. The frozen
  C0 root-only mode remains a separate historical method, not this fallback.
- E1's known endpoint representation failure remains unsupported. No epsilon
  relaxation, float64 global conversion or parametric endpoint redesign here.
- All four demos must receive an explainable result; none is required to pass.
  A policy rejection is not evidence of a treatment-introduced intersection.

## Research versus implementation claims

The proposed ideal theorem requires original and replacement patches to be
oriented PL disks with the same full, injectively identified boundary and the
same prescribed attachment to retained simplices. Their boundary-relative disk
homeomorphism preserves interface topology; interface validity additionally
requires the original interface to be valid. Whole-mesh embedding additionally
requires retained geometry to be embedded. Ideal temporal continuity and the
actual floating-point implementation require separate statements and checks.

Old C0 artifacts remain evidence for their exact-once, identity and limited
regression checks. They are not complete all-neighbour/all-time defect proofs.
Equal defect totals or equal intersecting-partner sets are insufficient.
