## Admission and stopping semantics

- PASS only means the stated gate's evidence was actually checked.
- Exhaustion of the complete, declared finite selector domain can reject
  this construction policy; it does not prove no other repair exists.
- A fully compiled fixed support violating a required source/interface/
  graph-fan condition must carry a concrete witness and can be rejected early.
- Incomplete search, missing metadata, budget exhaustion, unsupported code,
  unverified source-to-native identity and insufficient separating-plane
  searches remain UNKNOWN/UNSUPPORTED. AABB overlap is not collision proof.
- Continuous ideal geometry and actual binary32 realization are distinct.
  No ideal-only check authorizes actual mesh publication.
- If candidates survive source gates, validate real native requested queries,
  all owners, actual vertex identities, the actual proposed patch, full
  retained geometry including other elements, unchanged exterior, and atomic
  requested-schedule fallback before counting them as runtime admitted.
- A decisive necessary-gate failure may short-circuit later gates. If all
  events are decisively rejected with no unknowns, report policy admission
  0/131 and runtime attempts 0, not an empirical runtime failure rate of 100%.
- Do not stop at a newly encountered implementation gap while a safe, relevant
  way to close it remains. Do not lower gates to obtain a positive numerator.

Report certified admission / 131, proven policy rejections / 131, unknown /
131, conditional rates where meaningful, method-window natural hits and actual
modified frames separately. If unknowns remain, give bounds and causes rather
than a misleading point estimate. Event and same-root-group rates must be
labelled separately.
